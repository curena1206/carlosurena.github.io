The product decisions behind the prototype

I defined the customer situations, product requirements, financial trade-offs, commercial hypotheses and acceptance criteria. Implementation was AI-assisted. The work demonstrates how I connect customer value, product choices and provider economics, with explicit boundaries around what the evidence supports.

The central trade-off: recommending existing EUR liquidity can help a customer avoid an unnecessary conversion, even when conversion could create a hypothetical fee opportunity. The commercial question is whether useful, transparent decision support can sustain a licensing proposition.

What is working, simulated and assumed

The interactive prototype uses saved TrueLayer sandbox account and transaction evidence, synthetic corporate and merchant scenarios, and simulated payment outcomes. Commercial prices, costs and adoption are illustrative assumptions. Historical Stripe sandbox evidence is unconfirmed. No live account connection, actual funds movement, acquiring or settlement is provided. This is an evolving portfolio project, not a production financial service.

Current focus: independent usability review, clearer Commercial conclusions and validation of the customer proposition. Additional integrations and pricing models remain future work.
