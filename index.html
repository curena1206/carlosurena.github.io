Portfolio Economics

A payments business is more than the utility that processes a transaction.

Volume and revenue are visible. They do not fully explain how the business creates value.

A portfolio view shows how pricing and revenue mix respond to client behavior. It also reveals the effects of liquidity, servicing complexity, infrastructure dependencies, and investment priorities. Together, these conditions determine whether activity becomes sustainable commercial value.

This is the distinction between managing payment products and understanding the economics of a payments franchise.
